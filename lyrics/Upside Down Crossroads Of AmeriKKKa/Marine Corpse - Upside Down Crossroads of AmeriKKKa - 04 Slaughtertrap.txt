Corridors of ichor
Stink of putrid flesh 
Ammonia makes us sicker 
Scald our every breath

LIFE
Is this LIFE?

A perfect realm of torture 
Within a crowded cell
No light, no movement
A living hell

Rows of haunted faces
Search for any clue
Why have I been brought here? 
What do you intend to do?

Smell of terror all around 
Panic fills the air
Limits of my mind are stretched
I’m overwhelmed by fear
My life designed from birth to death
and circumscribed in steel
Refuse to see my suffering 
Yet deny I still feel

We choose 
They die 
Ignore 
Their cries

Shut out 
From sight 
All mercy 
Denied
